ProFactura 2.11.0

ProFactura updates itself: it offers each new version when you sign in. To install by hand:

Download ProSync-Setup-2.11.0.0.exe

What's new

  • The bars leave the ledger as the saw cuts them: every few minutes, any open ProSync reads the stations' scans - a bar of the saw's file is taken from the shelves when its first piece is scanned cut, and what is left of it once its last piece is cut is kept as a remainder, by its length. A window's steel at the steel saw and a damaged piece cut again come from the remainders first, else from a new bar; a scan taken back gives everything back. The shelves show each article's remainders and its metres; Remainders… lists them, adds one measured by hand, takes one out with its reason. The steel is keyed by Ramasoft's article - one steel, one key, whatever codes it is bought under - and a monobloc roller shutter no longer counts its slats twice.
  • The damaged step at every station: Damaged… shows the phone's form with the last label filled in - the photo is the proof - and the window is held at every station after the saw until the office decides under Problems: cut again, repaired, replaced. At the last station a checklist is ticked before the window goes on - five lines, or the office's own - and the ticks stay with the window's scan.
  • FenStock has a page Deliveries: the office's purchase orders in Ramasoft as they are written, received line by line and pane by pane at a PC or on the phone, with the delivery note's number and a photo where something is short or damaged. A receipt is never changed - an Admin corrects it with a receipt of its own, with its reason. The queue counts what came as on the shelves and stops waiting for it; a pane received broken waits under Glass to be ordered again. The phone's pages stand in two columns on a tablet. The box must be redeployed.

ProFactura 1.30.0

ProFactura updates itself: it offers each new version when you sign in. To install by hand:

Download ProSync-Setup-1.30.0.exe

What's new

  • The quote's discount on the proforma and the invoice, as Ramasoft's devis prints it: every discounted window shows its gross price, the percent taken off and the net, and the totals open with the gross total and what the discount took off - on the PDF, in the Excel and in the document's window. A document without a discount prints as before.
  • Schema 26: the line keeps the gross price. Documents made before this version show no discount; the ones made from now on do.

ProFactura 2.37.0

ProFactura updates itself: it offers each new version when you sign in. To install by hand:

Download ProSync-Setup-2.37.0.0.exe

What's new

  • Preparation of orders: a fourth tab, Accepted, beside All, White only and In colour. It lists only the customers' orders accepted and ordered, of those the list reads for the period chosen, and says how many they are.
  • The tab wears the violet of the accepted state: a violet shade with a fine violet line round its name while another tab is in front - it is found without being opened - and, once in front, the outline round it and its list in violet.
  • On that tab the tick Only those to accept has nothing to say and is greyed. No change to the database. No box to deploy.

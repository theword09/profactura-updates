ProFactura 1.26.0

ProFactura updates itself: it offers each new version when you sign in. To install by hand:

Download ProSync-Setup-1.26.0.exe

What's new

  • A sixth column on the board: Dispatched - the pallets out on a truck, each with what rides on it, whose it is and how long it has been out. Open one for its journey and the three words that end a trip: Delivered, Turned back, Unloaded. Delivered moves its productions on and sends it to the Delivered record; the other two put it back at the palletization.
  • The line's order is kept: a window is refused at a station until it has passed every station the workshop has before it - nothing goes from the saw straight to the pallet with the welding, the hardware and the glass never recorded. The station says which step is missing. The hardware station stays free to work before every sash is welded and cleaned, and the rule can be let go on the Sent to the machine page.
  • ProTrace can follow ProSync's Kaban driver alone: only the productions whose file it sent are on the board - nothing made in Ramasoft before, nothing another driver sent. Nothing is deleted, and the switch turns back at any time; it sits beside the queue switch on Sent to the machine and is turned on once.

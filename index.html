What's new

  • New: your company's logo on FenFlow. Every station shows the manufacturer's logo at the right end of its header, and so does the band at the top of every window the station opens: its settings, the checklist, the projects to cut, the accessories' photos, the choice of scanner, a project position by position.
  • It is the logo Ramasoft keeps with your company, the same as on the main menu. A station that starts without the network shows the one it read last.
  • Two of the station's windows have no band at the top and so carry no logo: the code for the phone and the reception of a material order.
